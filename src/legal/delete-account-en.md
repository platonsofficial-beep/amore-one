# Delete your ONE account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time.

## In the app
1. Sign in to ONE.
2. Open **Profile** (or **Settings → Απόρρητο**).
3. Optionally tap **"Λήψη των δεδομένων μου"** to download a copy of your data first.
4. Tap **"Διαγραφή λογαριασμού"**, type **ΔΙΑΓΡΑΦΗ** and confirm.

If you are the only owner of a venue, you must first make someone else an owner or close the venue.

## Without app access
Email [FILL IN: privacy email] from your account's address, with the subject "Delete account". We reply within 30 days.

## What is deleted
- Sign-in data (email, password, sessions) is deleted or anonymised immediately.
- Your name, email and photo are removed from your teams.
- Notifications and push devices are deleted.

## What may be kept
Records that belong to your employer venue (shifts, attendance, leave, team messages) stay with the venue in **anonymised** form, according to the venue's retention policy and the law. Backups expire automatically within [FILL IN: days].
